## Overview

This topic studies data-driven monitoring and prediction for carbon-negative biomass material manufacturing. Ambient drying is attractive because it is low-cost, scalable, energy-efficient, and able to preserve structural integrity, but its drying kinetics can vary significantly across material compositions and process conditions. Our work uses a custom real-time gravimetric sensing setup and a hierarchical nonlinear mixed-effects model to capture both population-level drying trends and sample-specific heterogeneity. By incorporating cellulose content, fan speed, and Bayesian updating from live measurements, the framework improves drying prediction and provides interpretable insight into how composition and airflow shape drying rate, moisture retention, and future process control.
