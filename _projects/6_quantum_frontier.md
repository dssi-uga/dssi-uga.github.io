## Research Focus

As distributed manufacturing becomes more connected, future systems will need security models that remain resilient in a post-quantum computing landscape. DSSI is exploring quantum-safe federated manufacturing and post-quantum-secure decision chains as an emerging research frontier.

## Applications

- Secure coordination for distributed manufacturing networks.
- Post-quantum-safe privacy and integrity guarantees.
- Verification layers for long-lived manufacturing data and decisions.

## Impact

This frontier connects the lab's trustworthy AI and secure manufacturing agenda to the next generation of cyber-resilient production systems.
