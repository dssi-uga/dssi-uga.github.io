Dr. Sun develops trustworthy, verifiable AI for advanced and smart manufacturing — additive manufacturing, quality engineering, privacy-preserving machine learning, and safe human–robot collaboration. The lab's work has been supported by NSF, USDA, NIOSH, the U.S. Navy, Honda, MxD, and GE. Dr. Sun is an Associate Editor of *IISE Transactions* and *ASME JCISE*, serves on the *Journal of Quality Technology* editorial board, and chaired the INFORMS QSR Section.
