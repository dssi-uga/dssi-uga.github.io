The **Data Science and System Informatics (DSSI) Lab** builds **trustworthy, verifiable AI for advanced and smart manufacturing** — from process modeling, monitoring, and control to privacy-preserving machine learning and safe human–robot collaboration. We work across additive and sustainable manufacturing, quality engineering, uncertainty quantification, and manufacturing cybersecurity, turning data into decisions that are accurate, uncertainty-aware, private, and safe enough to deploy on the factory floor.

Dr. Hongyue Sun is an Associate Professor of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Georgia and Director of the DSSI Lab. The lab's research has attracted **over \$9.7M** in funding from NSF, USDA, NIOSH, the U.S. Navy, Honda, MxD, and GE, including the **\$2.3M NSF Future Manufacturing (FMRG)** award behind the STREAM platform for multi-stage distributed manufacturing. Recognitions include the **SME Outstanding Young Manufacturing Engineer Award**, the University at Buffalo **Exceptional Scholar–Young Investigator Award**, two **IISE Transactions Feature Papers**, and an **ASME Member Spotlight**. Dr. Sun serves as **Associate Editor of IISE Transactions and ASME JCISE**, on the editorial board of the **Journal of Quality Technology**, and chaired the **INFORMS Quality, Statistics, and Reliability (QSR) Section**.

Before UGA, Dr. Sun was an Assistant Professor at the University at Buffalo. Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering and M.S. in Statistics from Virginia Tech; B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Beijing Institute of Technology.

**We are recruiting.** Motivated PhD, MS, and undergraduate researchers interested in trustworthy AI for manufacturing, smart and additive manufacturing, privacy-preserving machine learning, and safe human–robot collaboration are encouraged to [reach out](/teaching/) with a CV and a short statement of interest.
