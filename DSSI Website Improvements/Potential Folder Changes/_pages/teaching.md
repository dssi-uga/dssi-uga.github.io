## We are recruiting

The DSSI Lab is recruiting motivated researchers interested in **trustworthy AI for manufacturing, smart and additive manufacturing, privacy-preserving machine learning, and safe human–robot collaboration.**

- **Prospective PhD students** — email Dr. Sun (`hongyuesun [at] uga.edu`) your CV, transcripts, and a short statement of interest. Mention the thrust(s) you want to work on.
- **Current UGA undergraduates** — year-round research opportunities, including First-Year Research Experience projects through the College of Engineering.
- **Visiting scholars & postdocs** — reach out with a CV and a research statement.
