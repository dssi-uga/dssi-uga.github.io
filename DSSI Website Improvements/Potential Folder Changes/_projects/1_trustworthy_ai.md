The lab's through-line: AI that is accurate, **uncertainty-aware, private, and safe enough to deploy** on the factory floor. We develop uncertainty quantification, out-of-distribution detection with coverage guarantees, and verified decision chains so that data-driven models can be trusted in high-stakes manufacturing settings.

**Representative directions**

- Out-of-distribution detection with statistical coverage guarantees
- Uncertainty quantification for process models and digital twins
- Verified, auditable decision chains for autonomous manufacturing
