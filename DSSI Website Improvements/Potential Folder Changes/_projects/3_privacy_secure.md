Manufacturing data is sensitive and often siloed across partners. We build **privacy-preserving and secure** pipelines — federated learning, homomorphic encryption and secure multi-party computation, and zero-trust decision chains — alongside **ethical / illegal-product detection** and manufacturing-cybersecurity testbeds.

**Representative directions**

- Ethical fabrication: privacy-preserving detection of high-risk / illegal printed parts (EthicalFab)
- Homomorphic and multi-party computation for cross-site manufacturing decisions
- Hybrid cyber-physical testbeds for manufacturing security (NSF CICI living lab)
