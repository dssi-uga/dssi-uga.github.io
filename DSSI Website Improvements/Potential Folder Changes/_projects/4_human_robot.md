People and robots increasingly share the manufacturing floor. We develop **continuous safety modulation**, embodied AI, and autonomous robotic inspection and disassembly, grounded in occupational-safety analytics from wearable sensing.

**Representative directions**

- Safety-aware out-of-distribution detection for RL-based robotic inspection
- Autonomous robotic scanning frameworks for quality inspection (Autoinspector)
- Wearable-sensor analytics for worker fatigue, risk, and recovery
