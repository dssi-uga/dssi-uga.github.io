Our signature future direction: **quantum-safe federated manufacturing**. As manufacturing decision-making becomes distributed and data-rich, the cryptographic guarantees underneath it must survive the post-quantum era. We are building **post-quantum-secure decision chains** that combine privacy-preserving learning with quantum-resilient security primitives.

**Representative directions**

- Quantum-safe federated learning for multi-site manufacturing
- Post-quantum-secure decision chains and verifiable computation under adversarial uncertainty
