We design manufacturing processes that are **sustainable by construction** — carbon-negative biomass processing, climate-smart manufacturing, and life-cycle-aware process design — with data-driven drying, recycling, and quality assurance.

**Representative directions**

- Smart ambient drying for carbon-negative biomass manufacturing
- Life-cycle analysis for recyclable composite and fiber-spinning processes
- Climate-smart manufacturing (Honda / Honda USA Foundation)
