We model, monitor, and control advanced manufacturing processes — **direct ink writing, inkjet, and freeze-nano printing** — and coordinate them across **multi-stage, distributed systems** via the STREAM digital-twin platform.

**Representative directions**

- System-level multi-stage modeling and multi-criteria optimization of direct ink writing
- Online anomaly detection and process control from streaming sensor and video data
- Cyber-coordinated digital twins for distributed production (STREAM, NSF FMRG)
