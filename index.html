Data Science and System Informatics Lab

Trustworthy AI for Advanced Manufacturing

The Data Science and System Informatics (DSSI) Lab at the University of Georgia builds trustworthy, verifiable AI for advanced and smart manufacturing. We connect process modeling, monitoring, and control with privacy-preserving machine learning, uncertainty-aware decision pipelines, and safe human-robot collaboration.

Dr. Hongyue Sun is an Associate Professor of Mechanical Engineering and Director of the DSSI Lab. The group develops trustworthy AI methods for additive manufacturing, quality engineering, privacy-preserving computation, manufacturing cybersecurity, and occupational safety. This work has attracted more than $9.7M in funding from NSF, USDA, NIOSH, the U.S. Navy, Honda, MxD, and GE.

Dr. Sun serves as Associate Editor of IISE Transactions and ASME Journal of Computing and Information Science in Engineering, serves on the editorial board of the Journal of Quality Technology, and chaired the INFORMS Quality, Statistics, and Reliability Section from 2023 to 2025. Recognitions include the SME Outstanding Young Manufacturing Engineer Award, the University at Buffalo Exceptional Scholar - Young Investigator Award, and two IISE Transactions Feature Papers.

We are recruiting. Motivated PhD, MS, undergraduate, visiting scholar, and postdoctoral researchers interested in trustworthy AI for manufacturing, smart and additive manufacturing, privacy-preserving machine learning, and human-robot collaboration are encouraged to contact Dr. Sun at hongyuesun@uga.edu with a CV and short statement of interest.
